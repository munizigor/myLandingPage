# **App Name**: Equilíbrio Digital

## Core Features:

- Expertise Display: Showcase the IT expert's professional experience.
- Skills Spotlight: Highlight key data-related skills and certifications.
- Contact Gateway: Include a contact form for potential clients.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Muted Sage Green (#9BB773), representing growth and harmony, a subtle reference to wood energy (Moku). Chosen for a calming and professional feel.
- Background color: Light Beige (#F2EAD3), providing a neutral and balanced backdrop.
- Accent color: Soft Gold (#C8B486), used sparingly for highlighting key elements and CTAs. Adds a touch of luxury while maintaining a connection to earth energy (Tsuchi).
- Use clear and readable fonts that are appropriate for the tech industry, ensuring a balance between modernity and tradition.
- Employ a minimalist layout with balanced sections, reflecting Taoist principles of simplicity and natural flow. Use whitespace to create a sense of calm and clarity.
- Incorporate subtle, nature-inspired icons related to data (e.g., trees, water droplets) to maintain thematic consistency.
- Use smooth, gentle transitions for elements, creating a sense of natural movement and refinement without distraction.
